# SO-101 arm inputs for the gripper (2026-09-22)

Source CAD and URDF: `cad/so101/` (TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100 @ `eecbe3e`, Apache-2.0; see `cad/so101/SOURCE.md`). Measurements: `cad/so101/measure_interface.py`, run with `uv run --with cadquery python cad/so101/measure_interface.py` (about 35 s; prints a JSON report).

Tags: **[S]** sourced (URL or upstream file), **[D]** derived by the script from upstream CAD/URDF, **[A]** assumed. CAD dimensions are nominal model values, not measurements of printed parts.

## Interface frame

Frame **I** (mm): origin = centre of the 4-screw horn pattern, on the plane of the stock gripper's lowest face (hub rim). +Z = wrist-roll axis, pointing from servo 5 into the gripper. X, Y = parallel to the sides of the square screw pattern; Y is parallel to the stock gripper-servo (motor 6) axis, and +Y is the side where servo 5's long body extends.

The stock gripper (`Wrist_Roll_Follower_SO101`) attaches **only** to the single horn of servo 5 with 4× M3×6 screws [S: [LeRobot SO-101 guide](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/so101), "Gripper / Handle" and "Joint 5: install only one motor horn"]. Nothing else carries the gripper: every gripper load goes through that horn and servo 5's output bearing.

### Keep-out: stationary parts around the rolling gripper

The wrist link (`Wrist_Roll_Pitch_SO101`) rises past the mating plane on the +Y side (angles 38°–149° from +X), up to z = +5.3. Because roll travel is 320°, treat it as a full ring. Horn excluded (it rolls with the gripper).

|---|---|---|---|
| −3 … −2 | 11.0 | servo-5 output boss | D |

| −1 … 0 | 12.6 | wrist link | D |
| 0 … +1 | 12.6 | wrist link lip | D |

**Rule:** a rolling part stays inside **Ø24 (r ≤ 12.0) for z ≤ +2** and inside **Ø30 (r ≤ 15.0) for z ≤ +5.5**. It may widen only above z = +5.5, which is 4.5 mm above the horn face H.

### Static holding torque, worst case (fully extended horizontally, stock COMs from the URDF) [D]

| Joint | Arm-only torque | Stock-gripper torque | Lever to horn face / to a point 40 mm past it | Max gripper + cloth mass at 40 mm, by servo limit |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| shoulder_lift | 0.527 N·m | 0.328 N·m | 308.6 / 348.6 mm | C001 6 V stall 319 g · C001 7.4 V stall 405 g · C001 7.4 V rated **0 g** (the arm alone exceeds rated) · C018 12 V rated 133 g · C018 stall 706 g |
| elbow_flex | 0.235 | 0.218 | 196.0 / 236.0 | 598 · 725 · 110 · 322 · 1169 g |
| wrist_flex | 0.030 | 0.087 | 61.1 / 101.1 | 1601 · 1898 · 464 · 959 · 2936 g |

- Shoulder_lift is the binding joint. The stock arm at full extension needs 0.855 N·m (8.7 kg·cm). That is 45 % of C001 stall at 7.4 V and 53 % at 6 V.
- **Mass budget: match the stock loading** [D]. For the shoulder moment to stay at or below stock, a new gripper plus cloth may weigh at most 100 g with its COM 25 mm past H, 96 g at 40 mm, or 91 g at 60 mm (m ≤ 33,380 g·mm / (308.6 + d)).
- 50 %-of-stall budget at 40 mm [A: hold continuously at ≤ 50 % of stall, the same rule as `actuators.md`]: 82 g on the standard kit's 5–6 V supply, 125 g at 7.4 V, 276 g with C018 at 12 V.
- Published payload: the official README, the LeRobot guide and the Seeed wiki give no payload figure (checked 2026-09-22). One third-party page claims "~500 g" with no conditions [S: [roboticscenter.ai](https://www.roboticscenter.ai/hardware/so-101)]. It exceeds the derived 405 g stall limit at full extension on C001, so do not design to it.

## What the new gripper mount must match

1. **Bolt pattern:** 4× Ø3.2 clearance on a Ø14.0 bolt circle (9.90 mm square), with 4× M3×6 pan-head screws into the horn. `params.json` / `README.md` currently model **4× Ø3.2 on Ø20 with an Ø8 centre hole. That is wrong; change it to Ø14.0.**
2. **Clamp thickness 3.0 mm** from horn face H to the screw-head seat, with Ø6.0 head counterbores. An M3×6 under a 3.0 mm clamp leaves 3.0 mm of shank below H, which is 0.5 mm longer than the 2.5 mm-deep horn holes (z −1.5 … +1.0), so its tip reaches z = −2.0 [D]. The stock gripper uses exactly this screw and clamp, so the holes must be open at the bottom; confirm on the real horn. A different clamp thickness t needs an M3 × (t + 2.5 … t + 3.0) screw [D].
3. **Pilot recess Ø20.0 × 1.0 deep** over the Ø19.2 horn. It is the only radial locator (the screws are not), but with 0.8 mm diametral clearance it centres the stock gripper only to ±0.4 mm [D]. For a locating fit, model the recess at about Ø19.45 for FDM (19.2 + 0.05 snug + ≈ 0.2 print undersize, `tolerancing.md` §4) and confirm with a coupon [D/A]. `cad/gripper/params.json` → `wrist_interface` carries these values.
4. **Centre hole ≥ Ø5.4** so the horn centre screw stays reachable.
5. **Keep-out:** r ≤ 12.0 up to 1 mm above H, r ≤ 15.0 up to 4.5 mm above H. The `params.json` 32 × 32 mm square mount (corner r = 22.6) at the horn face would hit the wrist-link lip. Use a Ø24 hub, 4.5 mm tall, then widen.
6. **Mass:** gripper plus cloth ≤ ≈ 96 g with the COM 40 mm past H, to load the arm no more than the stock gripper does.
7. **Envelope:** stay inside the stock gripper's per-band envelope above, or re-check wrist-flex clearance against the forearm (motor-4 holder at z −104 … −76).
8. **Camera:** the stock gripper's M3 hex-nut camera points go away with it. Give the new housing an equivalent M3 mount if the wrist camera is kept.

## Open issues

- **STS3215 is a TTL serial-bus servo, not PWM** ("half duplex asynchronous serial" [S: Feetech]). `params.json` puts an STS3215 in the gripper, which conflicts with the 2026-09-22 ruling that the gripper actuator be PWM-controlled. Resolved in `docs/gripper/DESIGN-BASIS.md`: the gripper actuator is a PWM hobby servo; the STS3215 stays only in the arm.
- The CAD is nominal. Printed horn-recess and hole fits need a test print. Check `docs/gripper/research/tolerancing.md` for clearance allowances.
- Whether the team's arm is a standard kit (C001, 5 V) or a Pro kit (C018, 12 V) sets the payload budget. Check the servo labels.
