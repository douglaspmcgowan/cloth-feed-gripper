# Test equipment

Dated 2026-10-11. Prices are the vendor's listed price on that date [S] unless tagged [A]; shipping and tax are not included. The table is generated by `test_plan.py`.

## 0. Answer

- **Buy:** US$98.14 in total, US$77.20 without the spares (servo, FSR, printed spare parts). Two orders: SparkFun (load cell, HX711) and Adafruit (two thermocouples and amplifiers, spare FSR), plus the spare servo and a relay module.
- **Budget:** US$100 [A]. About three times the gripper's own BOM cost (US$30.35, `BOM.md`) and two R8 actuator caps. It covers what a teaching lab will not lend and the spares that the destructive tests (TP-13 stall, TP-15 step load) can ruin. It leaves under US$2 for the fallbacks below: if the lab lends nothing, drop the spares first.
- **Borrow** from the ME102B lab or the student shop [A]: balance, calipers, dial indicator, feeler gauges, bench supply, multimeter, oscilloscope or logic analyser, IR thermometer, slotted masses. If none can be lent, the fallbacks add US$60 [A] (US$158.14 in all, over the budget; see the alternatives table for the instruments with a cheaper substitute).
- **Build:** the pinch-force probe, a horn lever, a finger load saddle, a printed protractor and the fit coupons. Their drawings are in the procedures that use them (TP-01, TP-13, TP-12, TP-08, TP-11).

## 2. Notes

- **Adafruit 166** is the FSR in `BOM.md` (listed there as Interlink FSR 402, US$7.95). On 2026-10-11 the same product number is listed as an Alpha MF01A-N-221 at US$3.95. Its force curve may differ from the Interlink table that `fsr_model.h` fits; TP-01 measures the curve, so either part can be used, but the BOM line is out of date.
- **Load cell choice.** A 10 kg cell covers the 41 N stall-lever force in TP-13 and the gear step-load in TP-15 with one sensor; its 0.05 % FS error (0.049 N) is still small against 5 N. A 1 kg cell (Adafruit 4540, US$3.95 [S]) would be about ten times more precise at 5 N but would be overloaded in TP-13 and TP-15.
- **The probe reader** is a second microcontroller running `tools/probe/probe.ino` (HX711 and MAX31855 on one board, one CSV line per sample). It is separate from the gripper's ESP32 so the gripper firmware stays the build under test.
