Requirements: R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R7, R8, R11, M16, M20. Pass criteria (from VERIFICATION-MATRIX.md):

  • R1: Jaw opens and closes through the 1:1 sector pair; GSI confirms a 1:1 pair counts (M20). Pass/fail
  • R2: GSI answer that a PWM hobby servo counts as the DC motor, recorded in LOG.md (M20). Pass/fail
  • R3: `analogRead` trace of the FSR changes by ≥ 300 mV between 0 and 5 N during a grip [A threshold]
  • R4: Each of 5 presses logs one `EVT BUTTON_PRESS`; a 0.8 s hold logs `BUTTON_LONG`
  • R5: `arduino-cli compile --fqbn esp32:esp32:esp32` exits 0 on the tested commit; the board boots to IDLE
  • R6: No `delay(` in `firmware/gripper/`; the event log of one full cycle shows ENTER_ events in the section 8 order
  • R7: Signal is 50 ± 1 Hz, pulse 500-2500 µs range, clamped 1200-1900 µs by the firmware
  • R8: Receipt or vendor page price ≤ US$50.00 (BOM: DS3218MG US$12.99)
  • R11: Every [S] in docs/gripper has a public URL; none is a course file or pirated PDF
  • R18: Open stop +20 ± 1° (TP-08).
