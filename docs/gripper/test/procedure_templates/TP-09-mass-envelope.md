**What it proves:** whether the built gripper is light enough for the SO-101 shoulder (R10), clears the wrist when it rolls (R9), and has the {{tp09.moment_arm_limit}} moment arm the force and torque numbers assume (R17). The generated revision-current prediction below sets the safe reach. About 25 min, plus 10 min on the arm for the roll sweep. **H** is the mating datum of the wrist interface: the top face of the SO-101 wrist-roll horn, which the floor of the wrist plate's pilot recess sits on (`research/so101.md` row 1; z = +1.0 in frame I). On the gripper off the arm, H is the **recess floor** of the wrist plate. "H+4.5" means 4.5 mm above that face, toward the fingers. The lever from the shoulder_lift axis to H at full horizontal extension is 309.1 mm [D `design_basis.py`, MuJoCo; `so101.md` gives 308.6 from the URDF].

## Equipment

Lab balance (0.1 g, ≥ 500 g), steel ruler on edge as the knife edge, digital calipers with depth rod, a 3 mm dowel pin, a steel square, cigarette paper or printer paper (about 0.1 mm [A]), marker, the stock SO-101 gripper if the team still has it.

## Steps

**A. Mass (M14, M13).**

1. Assemble the gripper exactly as mounted: all fasteners (including the 4 horn screws, loose in a bag on the pan), FSR fitted, nail and backing bonded. Leave the servo lead on; coil the FSR leads and servo lead on the pan up to the length that stops at the wrist, and let the rest drape off onto a block at pan height.
2. Tare the balance. Weigh the gripper 3 times, lifting it off and replacing it each time: m_g.
3. Weigh the demo cloth (m_c, expect 13.5 g) and the FSR wire run from the gripper to the wrist tie point (m_w). Use m_w = max(measured, 5.0 g).
4. M13: weigh the stock gripper (follower body, jaw, servo 6, screws) 3 times. Record against 99 g.

**B. Centre of mass (M14).**

5. Fit the wrist plate with the jaw at the open stop (+20°; if the gripper is unpowered, set the jaw by hand to the open stop). Lay the gripper on its side across the steel ruler edge, roll axis perpendicular to the edge.
6. Slide it until it just balances. Mark the edge line on the housing. Measure from the recess floor (H) to the mark along the roll axis with the caliper depth rod: d_COM.
7. Lift off, replace, repeat for 3 balance points. Then, with no power connected, turn the gripper over by hand (other side down) for a 4th check; it should agree within 2 mm.

**C. Hub envelope (R9).** The hub must stay inside the stationary wrist-link lip (`so101.md` keep-out).

8. Measure the wrist plate hub OD with calipers in the band from its rim (H − 1, the recess is 1.0 mm deep) to H+1, at the first layer and at the top of the band: 3 readings, turning 60° each time. Record the largest: D1.
9. Same in the band H+1 to H+4.5: D2. Measure with the depth rod where the part first widens past D2: z_widen (should be ≥ H+5.0, `tolerance_stacks.md` fix 5).

**D. Moment arm r_c (R17).**

10. Push the 3 mm dowel through knuckle A's bore. Lay the knuckle and finger flat, pad face against the blade of the steel square, so the blade is the pad plane.
11. With the calipers parallel to the blade, measure from the edge of the dowel farthest from the tip to the fingertip end: L1. Then r_c = L1 − 1.5 mm (dowel radius) − 5.0 mm (pad centre 5 mm above the tip, DB section 3) = L1 − 6.5 mm. 3 readings. Repeat on finger B.

**E. Roll and flex sweep on the arm (R9).** Needs the arm; run it in session 3 if the arm is not on the bench now.

12. Mount the gripper on the arm. Torque off, or LeRobot at its lowest speed. Slip a strip of paper between the hub and the wrist-link lip.
13. Roll the wrist slowly through its full range (−157.2° to +162.8°, `so101.md`), then flex through ±95° at three roll angles (0°, +90°, −90°). The paper must slide freely everywhere. Look and listen for rubbing.

## Data to record

One row per item: m_g (3), m_c, m_w, m_stock (3), d_COM (3 + check), D1, D2, z_widen, L1 and r_c (3 per finger), sweep result. Columns: item, measured, unit, limit, pass, notes.

## Calculation

- m_total = mean(m_g) + m_c + m_w. d = mean(d_COM).
- **M = m_total · (309.1 mm + d)** [D, DB R10]. This lumps the cloth and wiring at the gripper COM, as DB does. Also report M' with the cloth at the pad centre (H to pad centre by tape) for information.
- Largest mass that passes at the measured COM: 43,720 / (309.1 + d) g.
- Safe horizontal COM reach from the shoulder_lift axis: **r_limit = 43,720 / m_total**. Record `r_limit` from the measured total mass and record the equivalent fingertip reach using the measured COM-to-fingertip offset.

## Pass / fail

- **R10:** U = {{tp09.moment_u}}. PASS if value ≤ {{tp09.moment_pass}} (limit − U); FAIL if value ≥ {{tp09.moment_fail}} (limit + U); INDETERMINATE in between. Stretch-goal PASS threshold: 33166 g·mm. CAD revision B predicts FAIL at full reach. The ruled disposition prohibits holds beyond measured `r_limit`; TP-14 enforces it.
<!-- mass-verdict:end -->
- **R9:** report D1 against Ø23.2 (fix 5) **and** Ø24.0 (DB R9); D2 against Ø29.2 and Ø30.0. Pass on each if the reading + 0.05 mm ≤ the limit. The sweep passes if the paper never pinches.
- **R17 (r_c ≤ {{tp09.moment_arm_limit}}, one-sided; U = {{tp09.rc_u}}, the whole r_c method: calipers 0.049 mm plus the square-blade contact ±0.05 mm and the dowel diameter ±0.01 mm [D `test_plan.py` `rc_mm`]).** Judge each finger on its own mean of 3 readings. **PASS** if r_c ≤ {{tp09.rc_pass}} (r_c + U ≤ 65). **FAIL** if r_c ≥ {{tp09.rc_fail}} (r_c − U ≥ 65). **INDETERMINATE** from 64.92 to {{tp09.rc_fail}}: measure again with a better gauge (a height gauge or the print shop's CMM, U smaller than {{tp09.rc_u}}), or ask the CAD owner to shorten the finger. The sign-off line is the worse of the two fingers. A short arm is never a fail of R17 (it only raises the servo torque needed, DB D3).
  - Worked example: r_c = {{tp09.rc_example}} with U = {{tp09.rc_u}} → 65.2 − 0.077 = 65.12 ≥ 65, so **FAIL**. The same reading with a coarse gauge, U = 0.3 mm → 64.9 < 65 < 65.5, so **INDETERMINATE** (not PASS, not FAIL): re-measure with a better gauge. Check any single reading with `python docs/gripper/test/tools/analyze_log.py judge --value 65.2 --limit 65 --u 0.077 --side upper`.
  - The old band (64.55-65.45 mm) is gone. It failed a part that was 0.4 mm short and passed one that was 0.4 mm too long.

## Hazards

- The gripper can roll off the knife edge: work over a towel.
- During the sweep a powered arm can pinch: keep fingers off the wrist joints; stop at the first rub.

## If it fails

| Symptom | Likely cause | Tell |
|---|---|---|
| Mass or COM differs materially from the generated revision-current prediction | Fabrication, wire, glue or geometry differs from CAD | CAD / DB owner |
| D1 or D2 over the limit | First-layer flare (elephant foot); fix with a chamfer or `efc` compensation (TP-11 item 4) | Tolerancing owner |
| Paper pinches in the sweep | Hub or housing hits the wrist-link lip | CAD owner |
| r_c off by more than 0.5 mm | Finger root seated wrong or wrong finger length printed | Finger / CAD owner |
