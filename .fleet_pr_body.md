## B40 coupling choice

At LC3, stall is 2.876 N·m, giving 410.9 N on one dog at radius 7 mm. Lash can leave only one dog loaded, so both the one-dog and two-dog cases are in `dog_check.json`.

| Candidate | One-dog bending MPa / SF | One-dog socket bearing MPa / SF | Two-dog SF: bend / socket | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| As-drawn M3 ISO 4026 45H in aluminium | 1,693 / 0.83 | Al 685 / 0.56 | 1.65 / Al 1.13 | Fails both one-dog checks. |
| Bare hardened Ø3 dowel | 853 / 1.99 | Al 685 / 0.56 | 3.99 / Al 1.13 | Fails one-dog aluminium bearing. |
| Ø3 hardened dowel in Ø6 steel insert | 853 / 1.99 | steel 685 / 1.31; Al 342 / 1.13 | 3.99 / steel 2.63 / Al 2.25 | Stress passes; matching the chosen 0.5 mm diametral slot clearance would widen the printed slot from 3.4 to 3.5 mm. |
| Chosen custom hardened M3×10 dog screw with smooth Ø2.9 shoulder in Ø6 steel insert | 944 / 1.48 | steel 708 / 1.27; Al 342 / 1.13 | 2.97 / steel 2.54 / Al 2.25 | Stress passes with the existing 3.4 mm slot; T14B-boss34 Cpk 1.10. |

The Ø22 horn leaves a 1 mm outer rim and a 0.5 mm web between each insert and the centre counterbore. Material allowables, custom shoulder dimensions, press-fit retention, root fatigue, rim and inner web strength are [A] pending a physical coupon. The hot-seat T14B case remains below capability and also needs a coupon. This PR is a draft until those assumptions and the derived prose/fixture updates are addressed.
