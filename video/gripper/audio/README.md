Procedural 62 s soundtrack (120 BPM music bed plus sound design) for the gripper film, synced to `STORYBOARD.md`.
Run: `python3 video/gripper/audio/make_audio.py` (needs numpy only).
Writes `video/gripper/build/audio/mix.wav` (48 kHz, 16-bit stereo) and `sync.json` (event times).
Fully procedural and deterministic (fixed seed): no samples, no third-party audio, nothing to license.
