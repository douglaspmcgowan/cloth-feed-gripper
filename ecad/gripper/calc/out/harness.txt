Budget = nominal plug voltage at hold inside 6.35 to 6.65 V, and every case between 4.8 and 6.8 V (the band is a hold-torque calibration band; stall is judged against 4.8 V only) [D].
20 AWG power pair MEETS the budget; a 26 AWG pair MISSES [D].
Corner finding: at the low set point and Fit A hold the plug is +32 mV against the 6.35 V band edge; the loop would have to be at most 114 mohm (is 90.5) [D]. The pigtail is 10.6 mohm of it; the unknown selected-servo lead is excluded.

Limit switch contact: COM + NO. (e) Flex
26 AWG silicone OD 1.5 mm [S]; minimum dynamic bend radius 10 x OD = 15.0 mm (absolute minimum 5 x = 7.5 mm) [S, D]; a service loop needs a diameter of at least 30 mm.
Jaw travel 30.2 deg (-10.2 to +20.0 deg, config.h JAW_MIN_DEG and DESIGN-BASIS R18); slack the lead must absorb at that radius: 7.9 mm [D].
Flex cycles to survive [A]: 100000 = 102.9 cycles/h x 972 h; basis: one open-close of the jaw bends the lead across the finger-B knuckle once; 100000 cycles = 102.9 cycles/h x 972 h (35 s cycle) [A, DESIGN-BASIS section 7].
One semester is about 10000 cycles (97 h) [A]; inspect every 10000 cycles.
