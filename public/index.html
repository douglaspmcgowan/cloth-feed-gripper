Cloth-feed gripper

Pinch cotton. Guide it into a sewing machine. Designed and simulated; not built or measured.

How to read this design ↓

Pinch, sense, feed

The cloth-feed gripper is a hand for the SO-101 robot arm: two fingers that pinch one layer of cotton and hold it under light tension while a sewing machine pulls it through.

  1. Close the fingers

    One servo drives both fingers through a pair of gears.

  2. Sense the squeeze

    A force sensor behind one finger tells the controller how hard it is squeezing.

  3. Let the cloth move

    The fingers hold light tension while the sewing machine pulls the cotton through.

It is a design for the ME102B course at UC Berkeley.
