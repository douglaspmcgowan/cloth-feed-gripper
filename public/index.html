A gripper that feeds cloth

Two fingers pinch one layer of cotton and feed it to a sewing machine. Designed and simulated, not built.

What it is

The cloth-feed gripper is a hand for the SO-101 robot arm: two fingers that pinch one layer of cotton and hold it under light tension while a sewing machine pulls it through.

One small servo drives both fingers through a pair of gears, and a force sensor behind one finger tells the controller how hard it is squeezing.

It is a design for the ME102B course at UC Berkeley.

Where the design stands

This design has not been built or measured. Every result comes from CAD, simulation and calculation, and the review marks which numbers are assumed.
