The recommended finger is 45 mm long with 6 ribs at 30°, a 0.8 mm wall, 1.0 mm ribs and a 4 mm tip, 3.64 g in TPU 95A printed flat.

It is solved as a 2D plane-stress model with quadratic triangles. The linear cases scale from a 1 N solution; the nonlinear cases use a large-displacement formulation and stop at the last load that converges, which is where the tip folds.

Torque at the mesh

The sweep covered 14,784 geometries: 6 materials, 4 modules from 0.8 to 1.5, 18 to 28 teeth, face widths 4 to 10 mm, two pressure angles and three profile shifts.

Each was checked for root and flank strength, contact ratio, undercut, wear and cumulative fatigue damage.

The virtual dyno is a DC-motor model of the servo behind its gearbox, driven by the same thresholds the firmware uses. It runs the feed cycle, a 30-minute thermal soak, four shock events and a fatigue ranking.

Shock cases vary the rotor inertia from 0.35 to 2.6 times the estimate because it has not been measured.
